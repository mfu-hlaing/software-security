How the pieces fit

In a class deployment, your browser connects through the class VPN to a private HTTPS entry point. The learning app serves explanations and simulations; isolated target services demonstrate vulnerable and defended behavior. Your own checkout is where you edit code, run scanners, build binaries and retain evidence.

Browser simulations are simplified models. The API’s identity header and chatbot’s deterministic replies are teaching shortcuts. Review and assessment weeks link the actual briefs; this practice does not replace your instructor’s submission process.
