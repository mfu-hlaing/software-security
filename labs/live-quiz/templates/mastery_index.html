How to use it with friends

Explain, predict, test, then compare evidence

Take turns being attacker, defender, and reviewer. Before each simulation or lab, predict the outcome aloud. Afterward, point to the exact evidence that confirmed or disproved it. The practice checks are private and ungraded, so retry until the reasoning feels automatic.
